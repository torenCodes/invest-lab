About This Site

This is a personal research platform built to support a disciplined, data-driven approach to stock investing. The goal isn't to predict the future — it's to identify stocks with real momentum, strong market interest, and favorable setups, and to do that research faster and more consistently than is possible by hand.

The philosophy here is simple: focus on green stocks only. Upward-moving tickers with real volume behind them. No short selling, no chasing noise. Just systematic screening, enriched with sentiment data from the investing community and professional-grade market feeds.

Every tool on this site is built from scratch, runs locally, and is maintained as a living project. This site is the front door to that work — and it will keep growing as new dashboards, research notes, and analysis are added over time.

⚠ Nothing on this site is financial advice. All content is for personal educational use only.
