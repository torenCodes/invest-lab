Lab Methodology and Track Record

How the lab works, and how its picks have performed.

The Invest Lab runs five proprietary screens each weekday focused on momentum, ETF consensus, deep value, insider conviction, and chart structure. Each codifying a well-known investment style, running it against fresh data so that no single lens is the answer. The goal is to provide signal-rich starting points for your own research and not a "buy this" recommendation.

When the same name appears across multiple dashboards, The Analyst takes over with a fundamental deep-dive that examines P/E, PEG, Free Cash Flow, Graham Number, ROE, and leverage before summarizing the findings with a written verdict.

Below are the receipts. Every top nominee is logged the moment it is flagged and graded on its own board's clock: a day trade at that day's close, a swing setup after 20 trading days, the long-term boards after 60. Each result sits next to the S&P 500 over the same days, and the misses stay alongside the wins.

Each board is graded on the clock it is built for. A Top Day Trade runs from the price when the scan flagged it to that day's close, or to the next day's close if it was flagged after the market closed. A Pattern Scanner setup gets 20 trading days. Underdogs, Tried & True and Insider Buying get 60, about three months. The S&P 500 is measured over the same days, starting from the last close before the pick. Typical pick is the median, so one huge winner cannot carry a board. Insider Buying is split at its August 2026 rebuild, so the current model is judged on its own picks rather than the old model's. Latest results are the most recent picks to reach their grading day, wins and losses alike. Hall of fame and Worst misses are the best and worst results since inception. Each result is recorded once on its grading day and never revised, so an older entry shows how the pick did then, not where it trades today. Picks still inside their clock are left out, and rows with fewer than 15 results are marked as early. Past performance is not predictive of future results. These are educational records of the lab's process, not investment advice.

About This Site

This is a personal research platform built to support a disciplined, data-driven approach to stock investing. The goal isn't to predict the future but to identify stocks with real momentum, strong market interest, and favorable setups, then do that research faster and more consistently than is possible by hand.

The philosophy is simple, put the focus on green stocks only. Upward-moving tickers with real volume behind them is what the system looks for. There's no short selling and no chasing noise, just systematic screening enriched with sentiment data from professional-grade market feeds and the investing community.
