🎯 Cadence Watchlist

A proprietary daily-rhythm ranking of the liquid US market, built to surface names with the kind of tradeable cadence you can learn and trade in and out of. The Cadence Score (0–100) blends average daily range, how consistent that range has been, liquidity, and price band, so a higher score means more reliable intraday movement. ADR% is the typical daily swing, Consistency is how steady that swing has been (closer to 1.0 is a steadier rhythm, lower is more erratic), and the Tag flags names whose price leans them toward swing territory. Click any column to sort.

🗺️ Sector Rotation

Where money is actually rotating across the eleven SPDR sector ETFs — measured against the S&P 500, not just on their own, since a sector up 1% while the market's up 2% is quietly losing leadership. The rotation map plots every sector by relative strength and momentum (Leading, Weakening, Lagging, Improving); the banner reads the overall risk posture from cyclicals vs defensives; and the table breaks out each sector's edge over the market across 1D–3M, whether it's rotating in or out this week, and the conviction (volume) behind the move. Toggle to absolute returns anytime. Sectors rotating in are often where the next high-cadence movers surface — click a row for today's scan movers within it, or a header to sort.
