🎯 Cadence Watchlist

A proprietary daily-rhythm ranking of the liquid US market, built to surface names with the kind of tradeable cadence you can learn and trade in and out of. The Cadence Score (0–100) blends average daily range, how consistent that range has been, liquidity, and price band, so a higher score means more reliable intraday movement. ADR% is the typical daily swing, Consistency is how steady that swing has been (closer to 1.0 is a steadier rhythm, lower is more erratic), and the Tag flags names whose price leans them toward swing territory. Click any column to sort.

🗺️ Sector Rotation

Where money is flowing right now, tracked across the eleven SPDR sector ETFs that carve the market into technology, energy, financials, healthcare, and the rest. Each row shows that sector's return over 1 day, 5 days, 1 month, and 3 months, so you can read at a glance which corners are leading and which are lagging. Sectors heating up are often where the next batch of high-cadence movers turns up, which makes this a useful map for where to go hunting. Click a sector row to expand today's scan movers within it, or click a column header to sort.
