## Monetization Paths

Given the setup (research dashboards, blog, individual investor audience), here are the most realistic options ranked by effort vs. return:

---

### 1. Broker/Fintech Affiliate Links — Best ROI, Low Effort

Brokerages pay $50–$200+ per referred account.

- **Webull, Moomoo, Tastytrade, Interactive Brokers** all have affiliate programs
- Add a subtle "Open a brokerage account" CTA on dashboard pages or blog posts
- Stock screeners like **Finviz Elite**, **Seeking Alpha**, **TradingView** also pay referral commissions

> Key: disclose affiliate relationships clearly (FTC requirement). Since the site already has a "not financial advice" disclaimer, adding "may contain affiliate links" is natural.

---

### 2. Display Advertising — Easiest to Start

- **Google AdSense** — no traffic minimum, starts immediately, low CPM (~$2–5 for finance)
- **Mediavine / AdThrive** — much higher CPM ($15–30 for finance) but require 50k–100k sessions/month
- **Carbon Ads** — developer/finance focused, better aesthetics, curated ads

Best placed on blog post pages where users dwell longer.

---

### 3. Paid Newsletter — High Ceiling, More Work

The blog posts (like the GTC recap) are already newsletter-quality research.

- **Substack** or **Ghost** — free tier + paid subscriber option
- Offer free weekly market summary; paid tier gets deeper analysis or early access to scan results
- Finance newsletters with small but engaged lists ($10–20/mo) can reach meaningful MRR with just a few hundred subscribers

---

### 4. Premium Dashboard Access — Highest Ceiling, Most Build

Gate some dashboard features behind a paywall:

- Free: current data with a delay or limited nominees
- Paid (~$9–15/mo): real-time results, email alerts when scans run, full nominee history

This requires an auth layer (Stripe + something like Supabase or Clerk). Meaningful work but recurring revenue.

---

### 5. Digital Products — One-Time, Passive

- A PDF guide ("How I built a personal stock scanner" or "Reading Insider Buying signals") sold via **Gumroad** for $15–25
- No ongoing commitment, blog traffic drives sales organically

---

## Suggested Starting Order

| Phase | Action | Time to Revenue |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Add broker affiliate links to blog + dashboards | Days |
| 2 | AdSense on blog pages | Days |
| 3 | Start a free newsletter (Substack/Ghost) | Weeks |
| 4 | Add paid newsletter tier once you have subscribers | Months |
| 5 | Premium dashboard gating (if newsletter validates demand) | 3–6 months |

---

## One Caution

Since the dashboards surface stock nominees, stay clearly on the "educational/informational tool" side and avoid anything that looks like personalized investment advice — that crosses into regulated territory. Affiliates, ads, and subscriptions to tools are all fine. Charging for "stock picks" is not.
