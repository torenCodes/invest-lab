Tried and True

Buy-and-hold research assistant. Scans the top growth ETFs from Vanguard, Schwab, iShares, Fidelity, SPDR, Invesco, and Baron — then surfaces the 10 stocks with the strongest cross-ETF consensus. Includes 3 & 5-year performance scorecards for each fund vs SPY.

The Analyst

Enter any U.S. ticker and receive a Wall Street-style fundamental analysis — pulling SEC 10-K & 10-Q data, computing the Graham Number, and scoring the stock as undervalued, fairly valued, overpriced, or speculative. Includes pre-scanned reports for AAPL, MSFT, and NVDA.

Insider Buying

Tracks open-market stock purchases filed by executives, directors, and officers via SEC Form 4. Surfaces cluster buys (multiple insiders buying the same stock), C-suite activity, and the biggest single transactions — a leading signal that insiders believe in their own company.

The Underdogs

Finds high-quality companies with temporarily beaten-up stock prices. Scores fundamental quality (margins, ROE, FCF), price drawdown from 52-week highs, RSI, and recovery signals like insider buying and earnings beats — sourced from quality ETFs like QUAL, MOAT, and COWZ.

About This Site

This is a personal research platform built to support a disciplined, data-driven approach to stock investing. The goal isn't to predict the future — it's to identify stocks with real momentum, strong market interest, and favorable setups, and to do that research faster and more consistently than is possible by hand.

The philosophy here is simple: focus on green stocks only. Upward-moving tickers with real volume behind them. No short selling, no chasing noise. Just systematic screening, enriched with sentiment data from the investing community and professional-grade market feeds.

Every tool on this site is built from scratch, runs locally, and is maintained as a living project. This site is the front door to that work — and it will keep growing as new dashboards, research notes, and analysis are added over time.

⚠ Nothing on this site is financial advice. All content is for personal educational use only.
